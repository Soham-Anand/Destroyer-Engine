# Materials

Material selection, compatibility, and test data for Destroyer-01.

## Selection criteria

1. Ethanol compatibility across the fuel path (no zinc, brass, or bare aluminium).
2. Fatigue and wear resistance at 7500 rpm and the 100 bar peak-pressure limit.
3. Thermal conductivity and distortion behaviour in the head and block.
4. Mass, cost, and manufacturing availability.
5. Galvanic compatibility with coolant and fasteners.
